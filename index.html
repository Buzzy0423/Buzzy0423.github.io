Publications

Z. Li, Y. Ling, Y. Gu, B. Huang, C. Liang, S. Islam, H. Bedri, J. Chirikjian, Y. Li, S. Nikolaidis, D. Seita. “FELT: Generating Tactile Signals from Vision for Visuo-Tactile Manipulation.”

Predicts tactile signals from RGB images, enabling tactile augmentation of vision-only robot demonstrations. The synthesized tactile representations improve contact-rich manipulation without requiring physical tactile sensors at deployment.

About

I'm a CS master's student at USC researching visuo-tactile perception and robot learning at the SLURM Lab. I'm interested in how robots can infer contact from vision and use it to interact more effectively with the physical world.

Before USC, I studied at SUSTech, where I worked on medical image enhancement, domain adaptation, and image quality assessment. I also interned at HiDream, developing diffusion-based image restoration methods. Outside of research, I enjoy photography.
